Source Catalog
==============

feedmosaic ships a curated registry of public publisher feeds, grouped by
editorial vertical. The list below mirrors :data:`feedmosaic.catalog.PUBLISHERS`
and records the publishers the aggregator is regularly exercised against. Each
publisher is linked to its home page; the parenthetical notes the kind of
coverage the source provides.

.. note::

   To propose a new source, add an entry to ``feedmosaic/catalog.py`` and open a
   pull request. Sources are accepted on the basis of a stable public feed and
   consistent topical coverage.
